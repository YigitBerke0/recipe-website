Cookie Recipe

Description

This is the best chocolate chip cookies recipe ever! Simple, straightforward, amazingly delicious, doughy yet still fully cooked, chocolate chip cookie that turns out perfectly every single time!

Ingredients

  • Flour
  • Salt
  • Butter
  • Sugar
  • Vanilla Extract
  • Eggs
  • Chocolate Chips

Steps

  1. Preheat oven
  2. Mix wet ingredients
  3. Add eggs and vanilla
  4. Combine dry ingredients
  5. Fold in chocolate
  6. Bake
